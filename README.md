# 🪔 Diwali Sales Analytics Dashboard
 
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Diwali season sales across products, age groups, zones, occupations, and gender.
 
![image alt](https://github.com/vaishnavi-selvamraj/DIWALI-SALES-ANALYSIS/blob/main/Screenshot%20(67).png?raw=true)
 
---
 
## 🔍 Overview
 
This dashboard provides a deep-dive into Diwali festival sales performance. It helps business and marketing teams identify top-spending customer segments, high-revenue product categories, and geographic zones driving the most sales — enabling better targeting for future festive campaigns.
 
---

| Metric | Value |

## 🚨 Key Insights
 
- **Food is the top revenue category** during Diwali — gifting and festive consumption drive high volumes
- **26–35 age group** contributes over 40% of all revenue — the core festive spender segment
- **Central zone** leads all regions at ~39% of total revenue
- **IT Sector and Healthcare** are the top-spending occupations
- **Female customers** appear to generate higher revenue than Male customers based on the gender chart trend
- Average of **2.49 orders per customer** suggests repeat purchasing during the festive window
